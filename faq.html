What is the typical timeline and delivery process for web and software projects?

We implement a battle-tested 4-phase agile engineering lifecycle to ensure transparent milestones and on-time delivery:

  1. Discovery & Architectural Planning (Week 1): In-depth business requirement scoping, technical stack specification, data architecture, and roadmap creation.
  2. UX/UI Prototyping & Design Review (Weeks 2-3): High-fidelity clickable wireframes, component design systems, and stakeholder approval loops.
  3. Agile Development & Sprint Demos (Weeks 3-8): 2-week sprint cycles with live staging previews, modular clean coding, and continuous CI/CD integration.
  4. QA, Security Hardening & Production Launch: Cross-browser stress testing, Core Web Vitals performance tuning, penetration testing, and zero-downtime deployment.

Standard corporate portals typically take 2-4 weeks, while complex full-stack web applications and SaaS platforms require 6-12 weeks.
