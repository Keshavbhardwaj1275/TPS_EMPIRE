This page explains how tpsempire.co.in uses cookies. Please read it together with our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a site. They help the site work, remember your preferences and give us information about how it is used.

Cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary: needed for navigation, forms and security. These can't be switched off.
  • Preference: remember choices such as language or details you entered before.
  • Analytics and performance: show us how visitors use the site so we can improve it. Some are set by third-party tools.
  • Marketing: measure our campaigns and show relevant ads on other platforms.

First-party and third-party cookies

We set first-party cookies. Third-party cookies come from services we use, such as analytics, embedded videos, social plug-ins, chat widgets and ad platforms. We don't control these, so please check those providers' policies.

How long they last

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies stay until they expire or you delete them.

Your choices

On your first visit, a cookie banner lets you accept, reject or customise non-essential cookies, and you can change your choice anytime. You can also manage cookies in your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data

Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

Do Not Track

There is no uniform standard for "Do Not Track" signals, so our site does not currently respond to them.
