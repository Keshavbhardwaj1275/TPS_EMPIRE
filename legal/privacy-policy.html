TPS Empire runs tpsempire.co.in and offers IT solutions and training: web development, cybersecurity, software consulting, UI/UX, digital marketing, business analytics, and skill-development programmes. This page explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It follows the IT Act, 2000, the SPDI Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Information we collect

What you give us

  • Name, email, phone number, company and designation when you fill in a contact, enquiry, quotation or registration form.
  • Educational details, résumé and course preferences when you enrol in a programme or apply for a job or internship.
  • Project requirements and messages you send while asking about our services.
  • Billing details for invoices. We never store your card or bank credentials; payments go through third-party payment providers.

What we collect automatically

  • IP address, browser, device, operating system, pages visited, time spent and approximate location.
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy).

How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and deliver the services, training or support you ask for.
  • To prepare proposals, run projects, issue invoices and manage client relationships.
  • To enrol students, communicate with them and issue certificates.
  • To process job and internship applications.
  • To send updates, newsletters and offers (you can opt out anytime).
  • To improve the site's performance and security, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

Consent

We process your data with your consent, to fulfil a contract or request, and for other uses the law permits. You can withdraw consent anytime by writing to us. This won't affect processing already done.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with:

  • Providers who help us run the site and business (hosting, email, analytics, CRM, payments, communication tools), bound by confidentiality.
  • Advisers, auditors, and government or law-enforcement bodies when the law requires or to protect our rights.
  • A successor business, if we merge, are acquired or restructure.

Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above, for legal or accounting requirements, or to resolve disputes. After that we delete or anonymise it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as access controls, encryption where appropriate and secure hosting. No internet transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You may:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to erase data we no longer need.
  • Withdraw consent or opt out of marketing.
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

Third-party links

Our site may link to other websites and platforms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Children

Our services are meant for people aged 18 and above. Students under 18 can join our training only with verifiable consent from a parent or guardian. If a child has shared data with us without that consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Data stored outside India

Some of our tools (such as cloud or analytics services) may store data on servers outside India, only as Indian law permits.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its revised date.
